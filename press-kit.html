Executive Summary & Curatorial Narrative

“She Is Rising from the North” is a landmark documentary exhibition, photographic monograph, and ongoing educational advocacy project chronicling fifteen years of transformative female leadership in Northern Kenya (Isiolo, Samburu, Marsabit, and Garissa counties). Created by acclaimed Kenyan documentary filmmaker and photographer Hildah Kathure in partnership with MOV Foundation (Stichting MOV, The Netherlands), the initiative reframes the public perception of Northern Kenya from a narrative of passive vulnerability to one of extraordinary resilience, intellect, and economic agency.

For pastoralist girls, secondary school is rarely a given. Severe distances, costly boarding fees, recurring climate droughts, and deep-seated pressures toward early marriage create profound barriers after primary school. Yet, through long-term sponsorship, 479+ young women have broken through these structural obstacles. Kathure's camera provides an intimate, dignifying witness to their trajectory—from the pastoralist rangelands to university lecture halls, surgical wards, and corporate boardrooms.

The Three-Act Structure of the Exhibition & Book

Act I: On the Grazing Fields

The pastoralist foundation. Life in Isiolo and Samburu; water journeys, livestock herding, and dreams quietly carried under the desert sun.

Act II: At School

The arrival at boarding secondary school. Discovering science, debates, and personal voice. Dreams transform into uniforms, certificates, and ambition.

Act III: On the Job

The graduates in society. Doctors, teachers, veterinarians, hospital CEOs, and entrepreneurs proving education is an engine of generational prosperity.

The Economic Case: Combating Poverty Through Education

Data compiled within the photo-book highlights that secondary education for a girl in Northern Kenya delivers an estimated 20–25% increase in lifetime earnings and fundamentally breaks the multi-generational cycle of early child marriage. The initiative advocates for three concrete policy and institutional priorities:

  • Targeted Secondary Boarding Subsidies: Eliminating the fatal transition gap between Grade 8 and Form 1 where most girls drop out due to boarding fee shortfalls.
  • Fairer Higher Education Financing: Reforming student loan criteria to prevent pastoralist university qualifiers from forfeiting admissions due to lack of upfront tuition.
  • 100% Proceeds Model: All sales of books and photo prints go directly and transparently into secondary school tuition fees for deserving girls from Isiolo and surrounding counties.
